# What is Salesforce?

Salesforce is a cloud-based CRM platform used to manage customers, sales, services, and business workflows efficiently.

---

# What is CRM?

CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management. It helps businesses manage customer data, communication, and business interactions in one place.

---

# Why Companies Use Salesforce

- Manage customer information
- Track sales and business activities
- Improve customer service
- Automate business workflows
- Increase productivity and efficiency

---

# What is an Account in Salesforce?

An Account in Salesforce represents a company or organization that a business works with. It stores details such as company name, address, and business information. For example, TCS or Infosys can be stored as an Account.

---

# Difference Between Account and Contact

An Account represents a company or organization.  
A Contact represents a person related to that company.  
For example, TCS is an Account and an employee of TCS is a Contact.

---

# Business Flow

## Lead → Contact → Opportunity → Customer

A Lead is a person who shows interest in a product or service. After communication, the person becomes a Contact, and the possible business deal becomes an Opportunity. If the deal is successful, the person becomes a Customer.

---

- Account = College
- Contact = Student
- Lead = Student Inquiry
- Opportunity = Admission Process
